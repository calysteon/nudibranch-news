Striped Nudibranch spotted washed up at Golden Gardens

The Striped Nudibranch (Armina californica) is usually described as a deep-water species you'd never run into on a beach walk. We found two of them stranded in the tidepools at Golden Gardens — and we have the photos to prove it.
